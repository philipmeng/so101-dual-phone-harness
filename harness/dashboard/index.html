01

Connect each phone

Open HEBI Mobile I/O on the same Wi-Fi as the Pi. Use the family and unique phone name shown on each arm’s card.

02

Get tracking ready

Hold the phone flat, screen up, with the top pointing forward. Let camera tracking settle. Release B1 and center A3 before enabling.

03

Move, release, recenter

Hold B1 and move the phone through space. Release it to hold the arm’s pose and reposition your phone. Each phone moves only its paired arm.
